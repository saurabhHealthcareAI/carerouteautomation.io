FAQ

Questions clinic owners ask us

How much does automation cost?

Every project is quoted after a free automation audit, so you pay only for the workflows you need. Most practices start with one workflow and expand once they see the time saved.

How long until it goes live?

A first workflow typically goes live in 2–4 weeks. Larger EHR or billing integrations can take longer. We agree the timeline with you before any work starts.

Is our patient data safe?

We design HIPAA-aware and GDPR-aware workflows: we use only the data each step needs, encrypt it in transit, restrict access by role and keep audit trails. We sign NDAs and BAAs where required.

Which systems do you work with?

EHR/EMR and practice management software, WhatsApp, SMS, email, Google Workspace, calendars, CRMs, payment gateways and spreadsheets. We connect them with tools like n8n, Zapier and Make, or directly through APIs. If you use something unusual, ask us.

Do I need technical staff?

No. We build, test and maintain the automations, and train your team in plain language. Your staff just use the results.

What support do we get after launch?

We stay with you after go-live: monitoring, fixes, small changes and regular check-ins to see what to improve next. Support terms are written into your proposal.

What if something breaks?

Workflows are built with alerts and a manual fallback, so your clinic never stops. If a step fails, we're notified and your team can carry on by hand until it's fixed.

Can we start small?

Yes, and we recommend it. Pick the one task that wastes the most time, such as reminders or intake forms, prove the value, then add more.

Will automation replace my staff?

No. It removes the repetitive parts of their day so they can focus on patients. Most teams find their work becomes calmer and more meaningful.
