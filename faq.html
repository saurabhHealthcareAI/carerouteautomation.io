Frequently asked questions

Cost, timelines, data safety and what it takes to get started.

How much does healthcare automation cost?

Every project is quoted after a free automation audit, so you pay only for the workflows you need. Most practices start with one workflow and expand once they see the time saved.

How long does it take to go live?

A first workflow typically goes live in 2 to 4 weeks. Larger integrations with EHR or billing systems can take longer, and we agree the timeline with you before work starts.

Is patient data safe?

We design HIPAA-aware and GDPR-aware workflows: we collect only the data a workflow needs, encrypt it in transit, restrict access by role and keep audit trails. We sign NDAs and BAAs where required.

Do I need technical staff?

No. We build, test and maintain the automations and train your team to use them.

Which countries do you work in?

We are based in Mohali, Punjab, India and work with healthcare teams locally and worldwide, remotely.
